# Isekai Store Anime Asset Brief

The app works without this pack by using the current public images and the
built-in comic SVG posters. These assets make the final episode feel bespoke.

## Illustration Pack

- `portal-poster.webp`: transparent or paper-backed anime portal splash, minimum 1600×1200, dynamic diagonal pose, electric blue portal, vermilion impact accents, no text baked in
- `shopkeeper-counter.webp`: transparent anime shopkeeper behind a cluttered counter, minimum 1200×1400, expressive “you are making a bad choice” face
- `portal-characters.webp`: two transparent character cutouts reacting to a portal, minimum 900px tall each, exaggerated poses, thick ink outline
- `item-potion.webp`: transparent magical potion, minimum 800×800, weird label and bright peach/red liquid
- `item-sword.webp`: transparent oversized dramatic sword, minimum 800×800, red accent
- `item-wand.webp`: transparent suspicious wand, minimum 800×800, violet accent
- `item-shield.webp`: transparent friendly shield, minimum 800×800, blue accent
- `item-elixir.webp`: transparent glowing bottle, minimum 800×800, acid-yellow accent
- `item-scroll.webp`: transparent teleport scroll, minimum 800×800, blue/violet accent

Preferred format is WebP or PNG with transparency. Keep each image visually
readable at a 180px card size.

## Texture Pack

- `paper-grain.webp`: subtle warm paper texture, tileable, 1200×1200
- `halftone.webp`: black dot pattern on transparent background, tileable, 600×600
- `ink-swipe.svg`: irregular hand-drawn brush stroke in black
- `burst.svg`: transparent comic impact burst with no baked text

## Audio Pack

Put these files under `public/audio/`:

- `episode-theme.mp3`: 20–60 second upbeat anime/fantasy loop, seamless if possible
- `portal-hit.mp3`: 0.5–1.5 second dimensional impact
- `dialogue-blip.mp3`: 0.1–0.3 second playful dialogue blip
- `item-reveal.mp3`: 0.5–1 second bright discovery chime
- `result-sting.mp3`: 1–3 second celebratory ending sting

Keep loudness comfortable and avoid copyrighted music. The UI starts muted if
the visitor chooses sound off and catches missing-file/playback errors.
